Our Story

How we got here

For almost two months, our entire relationship was basically Instagram likes. One mutual friend, two people on opposite sides of the world, and an impressive amount of not saying hello. Tanuja was in Bangalore. Prasid was in Seattle. Then, in early July 2024, while Prasid was in Chicago and having a particularly rough week, he finally did the brave thing: sent a message.

Things moved quickly after that. The conversation felt easy in a way neither of us had expected. We found the same things important, laughed at the same nonsense, and very inconveniently started liking each other despite both being deeply suspicious of long-distance relationships. So Prasid did the only reasonable thing: booked a flight to Bangalore without telling his family. Soon there was a first meeting, a trip to Goa, and the realization that this thing that had started with story likes was, in fact, very real.

There were more flights after that. Prasid came back to India. Tanuja spent seven weeks with him in the US. Somewhere between airports, long calls, ordinary days together, and increasingly fewer doubts, we built a life that stopped feeling long-distance and simply started feeling like ours. We also discovered one of our favorite ways to spend time together: Prasid on guitar, Tanuja singing, both of us happily losing track of time.

We made it official with a registered marriage in December 2025. This December, we finally get to do the loud, joyful version—with our families and friends around us. Consider this the celebration of a story already well underway, and of everything still to come.
